### Hi, I'm Eya 

MSc student in Biomedical Engineering (Erasmus Mundus EMMBIOME, University of Patras), with a BSc in Data Science from the American University in Cairo.

I work on machine learning for neuroimaging and biosignals: multimodal fusion, uncertainty estimation, and models that generalise across sites and subjects.

#### Selected work

| Project | Modality | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [ua-poe-autism-fmri](https://github.com/eyagammoudi/ua-poe-autism-fmri) | Resting-state fMRI | Uncertainty-aware Product-of-Experts fusion of multi-view brain connectivity for cross-site autism diagnosis (paper under review) |
| [UMM-NET](https://github.com/eyagammoudi/UMM-NET) | 3D MRI | Deep learning framework for brain segmentation in 3D MRI volumes |
| [brain-mri-segmentation](https://github.com/eyagammoudi/brain-mri-segmentation) | Structural MRI | Dense encoder-decoder networks for hemisphere and whole-brain structure segmentation |

| [mri-dementia-prediction](https://github.com/eyagammoudi/mri-dementia-prediction) | MRI-derived and clinical features | Predicting dementia progression from longitudinal data |
